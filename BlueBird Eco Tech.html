Why now

Vehicle import is a black box

THE PROBLEM

Sourcing, shipping, customs, compliance, and registration run through separate parties with no shared system. Importers can't see where a vehicle is, duty and fees are calculated by hand, and the import process hasn't modernized to keep pace with Vision 2030's hybrid/EV growth.

THE SOLUTION

One platform for the entire chain: Korean sourcing sync → GPS-tracked logistics via a UAE Free Zone hub → customs & duty calculation → SASO/environmental compliance → certification → registration-ready handover. One live number for cost, one tracked status per vehicle, one paper trail.
